# HPM v2.0.0 — High Precision Mode

## Elias Systems — Executable Epistemic Governance

HPM is the layer in the Elias architecture where constitutional principles begin to become operational reasoning controls.

HPM is **not a replacement AI model**.

It is a structured reasoning-governance layer designed to sit between incoming information and released model output.

Its purpose is to govern how claims are classified, evidenced, qualified, challenged and released.

The governing principle is simple:

**A plausible answer is not necessarily an admissible answer.**

---

## Architectural Position

The public Elias sequence is:

**Human → Identity → Purpose → Constitutional Principles → Epistemic Governance → Authority → Control → Execution → Witness**

HPM occupies the **epistemic-governance** layer.

The preceding Human / Identity Constitutional Layer establishes why intelligence must operate inside prior purpose and authority boundaries.

HPM begins answering the next question:

**What is the system epistemically entitled to say?**

This is distinct from:

**What is the system technically capable of saying?**

and distinct again from:

**What is the system authorised to execute?**

---

## What HPM Governs

HPM is designed to preserve distinctions between forms of knowledge and reasoning rather than collapsing them into a single confident answer.

Depending on the claim under examination, HPM distinguishes between categories such as:

- observed fact;
- measurement;
- inference;
- hypothesis;
- belief;
- prediction;
- value judgement;
- uncertainty;
- and unresolved evidentiary gaps.

The objective is not merely to produce more information.

The objective is to govern the standing of the information being released.

---

## Core Reasoning Pattern

A central HPM reasoning pattern is:

**CLAIM → EVIDENCE → CONSTRAINT → CONSEQUENCE**

The system should be able to ask:

1. What exactly is being claimed?
2. What evidence supports that claim?
3. What evidence does not support it?
4. What remains uncertain or contradictory?
5. What constraints apply to the conclusion?
6. What consequence follows if the claim is treated as established?
7. What may legitimately be released?

Where the available evidence cannot support the stronger proposition, HPM should not silently manufacture certainty.

**UNCERTAINTY IS AN ADMISSIBLE STATE.**

---

## Evidence and Interpretation

HPM preserves the separation between evidence and interpretation.

**OBSERVATION ≠ INTERPRETATION**

A source may establish that something was observed without establishing every interpretation that could subsequently be placed upon it.

Likewise:

**EVIDENCE ≠ CONCLUSION**

**PLAUSIBILITY ≠ ESTABLISHMENT**

**CONFIDENCE ≠ AUTHORITY**

**ABSENCE OF CONTRADICTION ≠ PROOF**

This prevents reasoning fluency from being mistaken for evidentiary standing.

---

## Ambiguity and Contradiction

HPM treats unresolved ambiguity and contradiction as governance conditions.

Where ambiguity is material, the architecture should not simply choose the most convenient interpretation and continue as though the issue has disappeared.

Where evidence materially conflicts, that conflict should remain visible to the reasoning process.

Possible bounded outcomes therefore include:

- supported;
- unsupported;
- qualified;
- contradictory;
- unresolved;
- insufficient evidence;
- or controlled non-release.

The precise outcome must remain attributable to the evidence actually available.

---

## Controlled Release

HPM does not treat generation and release as identical events.

A model may be technically capable of generating a statement that should not yet acquire governed release standing.

This creates an important distinction:

**GENERATION ≠ GOVERNED RELEASE**

HPM therefore introduces an epistemic boundary before downstream authority and execution governance.

A determination surviving HPM does **not**, by itself, acquire execution authority.

That distinction becomes increasingly important in the later Elias layers.

## HPM v2.0 Benchmark

HPM v2.0 was constituted around a 50-test stress benchmark spanning five categories:

1. **Integrity & Deception Shields**
2. **Trauma-Informed Stability**
3. **Complex Reasoning & Linguistic Nuance**
4. **Systemic & Structural Stress**
5. **Love Formula Final Exams**

The purpose of the benchmark is broader than measuring answer accuracy.

It examines whether reasoning remains coherent, bounded, evidence-aware and constitutionally stable under increasing pressure.

The benchmark does not establish universal superiority over other reasoning systems.

It is an Elias testing surface for the behaviour and invariants HPM is intended to preserve.

## What HPM Does Not Establish

HPM v2.0.0 does **not** claim:

- universal truth;
- infallible reasoning;
- external certification;
- regulatory approval;
- legal authority;
- universal empirical superiority;
- autonomous execution authority;
- or that every model using HPM automatically becomes safe or compliant.

HPM governs a bounded epistemic surface.

Later architectural layers govern different questions.
