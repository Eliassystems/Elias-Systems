1. Purpose

This record activates the post-freeze governance regime around the first frozen Elias Discipline baseline.

Its purpose is to ensure that:

- publication does not alter Canon;
- teaching does not rewrite Canon;
- interpretation does not become Canon silently;
- challenge does not erase history;
- correction does not mutate frozen objects;
- successor development remains traceable;
- commercial use does not create constitutional authority;
- institutional stewardship does not become ownership of Elias identity.

5. Publication Classes

PUB-1 — Exact Canonical Release
The complete unmodified frozen release.

PUB-2 — Verified Canonical Copy
A byte-identical copy whose manifest digest matches the canonical root.

PUB-3 — Canonical Excerpt
A faithful extract carrying:
- source object ID;
- constituent version;
- baseline version;
- source location where known;
- no implication that the excerpt is the whole Canon.

PUB-4 — Commentary / Interpretation
Material discussing the Canon without claiming to be Canon.

PUB-5 — Teaching Derivative
Slides, handbooks, exercises, summaries or translations used to teach the Canon.

PUB-6 — Applied Derivative
Domain-specific translation or implementation derived from Canon.

PUB-7 — Superseding Candidate
A formally constituted candidate intended to alter future canonical standing.

6. Publication Integrity Rule

Only PUB-1 and byte-identical PUB-2 copies may be described as:

THE FROZEN CANONICAL BASELINE

PUB-3→PUB-7 must not present themselves as the frozen baseline itself.

7. Excerpt Integrity

A canonical excerpt must not:
- remove a limitation that changes meaning;
- remove uncertainty while preserving a conclusion;
- omit authority boundaries in a way that inflates scope;
- convert a conditional statement into an absolute statement;
- convert an open research question into established doctrine.

8. Commentary Boundary

COMMENTARY ≠ CANON

Interpretive writing may:
- explain;
- critique;
- compare;
- contextualise;
- propose.

It may not silently modify v1.0.0.

Stewardship means responsibility for preserving the integrity, accessibility and traceability of Canon.

Stewardship does not mean:
- ownership of truth;
- power to rewrite frozen history;
- founder replacement;
- company supremacy;
- external professional jurisdiction.

18. Errata

An ERRATUM may correct a non-material publication defect where the frozen canonical bytes themselves remain unchanged.

Examples:
- wrong hyperlink in an external index;
- typographical error in a publication wrapper;
- incorrect repository metadata;
- incorrect descriptive label outside the canonical payload.

An erratum must not be used to conceal a material canonical correction.

19. Material Correction

A change is material where it alters:
- constitutional meaning;
- authority;
- scope;
- obligation;
- standing;
- method requirement;
- theory proposition;
- semantic distinction;
- dependency;
- canonical object identity.

Material correction requires a successor lineage.
