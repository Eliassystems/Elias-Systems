5. What SHA-256 Establishes

A matching digest establishes byte-level integrity relative to the declared canonical object.

It does not establish:

TRUTH
LEGITIMACY
EMPIRICAL VALIDITY
EXTERNAL CERTIFICATION
EXTERNAL AUTHORITY

Therefore:

HASH INTEGRITY ≠ TRUTH
