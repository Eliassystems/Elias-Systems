1. What the Elias Discipline Is

The Elias Discipline is the systematic study and practice of how identity, intention, observation, evidence, authority, governance, intelligence, execution and accountability should relate in human and technological systems so that capability remains constitutionally aligned with benevolent purpose.

Its governing question is:

How can a person, institution or intelligent system acquire and exercise capability without losing the identity, purpose, truthfulness, human agency and moral constraints that justify that capability in the first place?

2. Constitutional Ordering

The first frozen baseline preserves the ordering:

IDENTITY
→ PURPOSE
→ CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES
→ GOVERNANCE
→ INTELLIGENCE
→ EXECUTION
→ WITNESS

The discipline rejects the idea that downstream capability can create its own upstream authority.

3. Core Constitutional Propositions

The baseline includes the following governing distinctions:

IDENTITY PRECEDES AUTHORITY
INTENTION PRECEDES INTELLIGENCE
