# Why the Elias Discipline Exists

Eventually, the work beneath Elias stopped being only a collection of software systems.

It was confronting deeper questions:

**What is identity?**

**What gives authority standing?**

**How should observation be separated from interpretation?**

**What happens when evidence is incomplete?**

**When should an intelligent system be permitted to act?**

**How should consequential execution be governed?**

**How can a system remain challengeable, correctable and accountable?**

The Elias Discipline was constituted to make those questions explicit.

Its purpose is not simply to describe what an AI system **can** do.

Its purpose is to examine what it **should be permitted to do, under what authority, using what evidence, with what limits, and with what record of consequence.**

EBASE-001 v1.0.0 was frozen only after its defined internal constitution, integrity and examination process was completed.

It distinguishes capability from authority.
