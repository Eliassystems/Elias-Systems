## Availability Status

Publications may be identified using one of the following statuses:

### PUBLIC

Available for public reading or reference subject to the rights and conditions stated with the publication.

### COMMERCIAL LICENCE

Available under a separate commercial licence.

Public description or preview does not grant commercial, implementation or redistribution rights.

### PURCHASE

Available as a paid publication or e-book for individual use under the terms supplied with that product.

### ENTERPRISE LICENCE

Available for organisational, institutional or enterprise use under separately agreed terms.

### IMPLEMENTATION LICENCE

Covers authorised implementation, deployment or integration of designated proprietary Elias Systems material.

### EVIDENCE RECORD

Published to establish an inspectable public record.

Publication of evidence does not transfer ownership, implementation rights, validation authority, commercial rights or partnership status.

### COMING SOON

Publication has been identified for future release but is not yet commercially or publicly available.

# Commercial Books & E-Books

Elias Systems will make selected books and e-books available for purchase and licensing.

Each release entry will identify:

**Title**  
**Author**  
**Edition / Version**  
**Publication Date**  
**Availability Status**  
**Description**  
**Permitted Use**  
**Purchase or Licensing Route**

Paid publications themselves will not automatically be deposited into this public repository.

Where appropriate, this repository may instead contain:

- publication information;
- cover material;
- a description or abstract;
- selected preview material;
- version information;
- integrity information;
- licensing conditions;
- an official purchase or licensing route.

This allows the existence and provenance of a publication to remain publicly inspectable without making the licensed work itself freely distributable.
